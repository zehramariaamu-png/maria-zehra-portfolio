About the Researcher

02 — ACADEMIC PROFILE
MARIA ZEHRA

Between classical tradition and emerging realities.

I am a PhD Candidate in the Department of Islamic Studies at Jamia Millia Islamia, New Delhi.

My doctoral research examines crypto assets through the lenses of Islamic law, with particular attention to Bitcoin as an asset and its implications for Islamic legal and financial thought.

My broader academic interests encompass Maqāṣid al-Sharīʿah, Islamic financial ethics, contemporary jurisprudence, financial technology and the ethical implications of artificial intelligence.

I seek to bring classical Islamic scholarship into critical dialogue with contemporary socio-economic and technological developments.
